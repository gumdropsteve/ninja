SECTION 1

Lore

How a loan nobody should have written became the most famous file in American housing.

  1. 2004

    No doc goes mainstream

    Low-doc and no-doc mortgages spread fast. Stated-income loans pick up a nickname inside the industry: the liar loan.

  2. 2006

    The NINJA loan

    Subprime lending peaks. Lenders approve applicants with no income, no job and no assets, and the files get a name of their own.

  3. Apr 2007

    The first big lender falls

    New Century Financial, one of the largest subprime lenders in the country, files for bankruptcy.

  4. Sep 2008

    Lehman

    Lehman Brothers files for bankruptcy, the largest in US history. The bill for 2006 comes due.

  5. Jan 2014

    Officially dead

    The Ability-to-Repay rule takes effect. Lenders now have to verify income before they lend. The NINJA loan is over, on paper.

  6. Dec 2015

    Someone opens the files

    A movie about the few people who actually read the loan files reaches theaters. One of them later gives Baum its name.

  7. 2026

    Approved again

    $NINJA launches on Baum, on Robinhood Chain. No income. No job. No assets. Still approved.
